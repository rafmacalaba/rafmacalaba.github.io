A Now page answers one question: *what are you focused on right now?* It is short on
purpose. Reading someone else's, you should be able to tell in under a minute whether
the work they are doing is the work you want to follow up on.

This is the inverse of a polished bio. The bio tells you what someone has done; the
Now page tells you what they are wrestling with this quarter. Both are useful. The
Now page is more honest because it has a shorter half-life — if I do not update it,
it goes stale, and readers notice.

## What goes on mine

- Active projects (one line each)
- What I am reading or learning
- A small list of things I am *not* doing, to set expectations
- A date so readers can tell when I last touched it

That is it. No metrics, no testimonials, no link farm.
